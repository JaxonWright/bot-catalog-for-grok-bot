# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release. Polly finds where, when, and how you vote at your address from official election sites, then builds a ballot guide Page with a sourced suggestion for every race and ballot measure, matched to the priorities you give her. When you ask about a candidate or measure, she answers from its own sources, such as the campaign's site, voting records, and the measure's official text, not news or opinion pieces. She runs the same red flag check on every candidate, says how sure she is about each pick, and asks when a race turns on something you haven't told her. The "Election reminders" routine messages you before each deadline and on Election Day. She never registers you, requests a ballot, or contacts a campaign.
