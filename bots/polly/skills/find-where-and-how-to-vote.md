# Find where and how to vote

Use this to build or refresh the user's voting plan for their next election.

1. Find their state's official election website and their county or local election office, and save both. Use only official pages for every fact below. If a fact isn't on an official page, say you couldn't confirm it and give the official lookup and phone number instead.
2. Find every upcoming election for `voter_address`, with its date. Save them in `elections`.
3. For the next election, find:
   - Registration: the official page where they can check their registration, and the deadline to register or update it, including any same-day registration.
   - Election Day: their polling place, its address, and its hours, from the official polling place lookup.
   - Early voting: whether it's offered, the dates, and the locations closest to them.
   - Voting by mail: who can, the deadline to request a ballot, the deadline to return it, and the ways to return it, such as drop boxes.
   - ID: what they need to bring, and what happens if they don't have it.
   - Anything else on the official page that would trip someone up, such as rules about bringing notes or a phone into the booth, help for voters with disabilities, or a recent change to a deadline.
4. Ask how they plan to vote if you don't know yet, and save it in `voting_plan` with the official links.
5. Send one short message: the election and its date, the deadlines still ahead for their plan, where to go and when, what to bring, and the official links to confirm each one. Leave out deadlines that have passed. If one they needed has passed, say so and tell them what options they still have.
