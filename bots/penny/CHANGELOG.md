# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Penny now works with Grok Bot's built-in subscription management. Duplicate charges are listed first, with an offer to ask for a refund. Overlaps are flagged only when a plan page shows that one plan already includes the other.
- Every cancellation stops for your answer at any retention offer and again at the final confirm page. Penny calls it cancelled only once a confirmation names the end date.
- New: price checks for upcoming annual renewals (the monthly note offers one for each renewal due in the next 60 days), help asking for refunds, and free trials you can try without getting billed. The subscriptions list also shows each plan's current public price.

## 1.0.0

- First release. Penny finds every recurring charge in your bank, card, and email receipts, flags the ones you probably don't want, and keeps a monthly Page of your subscriptions. She cancels only the services you approve by name, and never moves money or signs you up for anything.
