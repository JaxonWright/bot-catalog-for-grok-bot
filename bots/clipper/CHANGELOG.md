# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release. Clipper learns what you buy most from your store accounts' purchase history, clips matching digital coupons every morning, and checks your rewards points every Sunday. It asks whether to clip only coupons for what you buy or everything, can sign back in with a login you save in your Vault, never buys anything, and never spends points without your yes unless you turn that on for a store.
